Typical scam techniques

* Bank account closure if you don’t enter credentials  
* Missed payments, need to log in and pay  
* Malware downloads in attachments  
* Free gift received, need to pay for shipping  
* Callback with phone number  
* Fake order confirmation with “refund” offered  
* Billing issues need you to update billing information  
* Prime membership confirmation  
* Tech care auto renewal, login to cancel  
* Account banned, login to appeal ban  
* Fake password reset  
* Free in game currency or store credit if you log in  
* Frozen debit card, need to enter information to reclaim account  
* Apple pay account frozen  
* Failed at\&t auto pay  
* Package couldn’t be delivered  
* Utility shutoff

Things to watch out for

* Misspelled domains (fed-ex.com or amozon.com)  
* Weird email addresses  
  * Normal gmail, hotlook, hotmail, yahoo, icloud, roadrunner rather than company email  
  * Random numbers, letters, or hyphens tacked on company domains or names  
  * Misspellings like paypa1.com  
* Aggressive timing and language

Normal emails which aren’t scams

* Shipping details  
* 2FA codes  
* Newsletter updates  
* Account statements

Password guessing game

* At least 16 characters long  
* Passphrase of 4-7 unrelated words is one good idea  
* Random numbers and mixed case letters is also good  
* Any combination is nice, as long as it doesn’t have personal information  
* Terrible ideas  
  * 123456 and so on  
  * Qwerty  
  * Azerty  
  * Password  
  * Guest  
  * Default  
  * Admin  
  * Secret  
  * Student  
  * Minecraft  
  * Welcome  
  * Memorable movies characters  
  * Sports teams  
  * Pet names  
  * Single repeated character  
  * Dictionary words  
  * Asdfgh  
  * Combination of two weak passwords  
  * Munged version of weak password
